01 / ABOUT

More than moderation. It's about trust.

A community is built by the people in it. The way they are welcomed, heard, and treated makes a real difference.

My interest in Web3 community management comes from understanding how much a strong community matters to a project's growth. Behind every active group are people looking for answers, sharing ideas, learning about a project, or trying to find their place in a new ecosystem.

I believe a good moderator should be approachable without losing professionalism, firm about community rules without being disrespectful, and attentive enough to recognize when a conversation needs guidance or an issue needs to be escalated.

I want to contribute to communities where communication is clear, members are treated fairly, and safety is taken seriously. I'm committed to learning each project's guidelines, understanding its culture, working with the team, and handling responsibilities with care and consistency.

My goal is to keep growing in Web3, contribute meaningful work, and help teams build communities that people are proud to be part of.

02 / SKILLS

The work behind a healthy community.

Effective community management takes patience, sound judgment, clear communication, and a willingness to help. These are the areas I focus on in my approach to the role.

✦

Telegram Moderation

A busy Telegram group needs more than someone who watches messages. It needs a moderator who pays attention, helps members find reliable information, applies the rules fairly, and knows when to step in. I focus on keeping conversations organized, addressing disruptive behaviour appropriately, and making newcomers feel welcome.

◈

Discord Community Support

Discord communities can have many channels, discussions, and different member needs. I value giving clear directions, helping people find the right channel, answering questions patiently, and keeping communication respectful. I also understand the importance of following server rules and working with other moderators rather than acting alone.

⌘

Community Safety

Trust can be damaged quickly when suspicious links, spam, impersonation, or misleading messages go unchecked. I take a careful approach to identifying concerns, following the project's security and moderation procedures, and alerting the appropriate team. Protecting members means acting responsibly instead of making assumptions or promises.

◎

Member Engagement

A healthy community gives people a reason to participate. I believe in welcoming new members, encouraging constructive discussion, responding thoughtfully, and helping members feel that their questions matter. Engagement should feel genuine, not forced, and should reflect the project's culture and values.

↗

Communication & Conflict Handling

Moderators often deal with confusion, frustration, and disagreements. I value staying calm, listening before responding, explaining decisions clearly, and treating people with respect. When a problem requires more authority or expertise, I believe in escalating it to the right person instead of allowing it to become a bigger issue.

◇

Commitment & Teamwork

Reliable community support comes from consistency. I aim to learn the team's expectations, follow instructions, communicate when something needs attention, and remain open to feedback. I'm committed to developing my skills, learning each project's community culture, and becoming someone the team can depend on.

03 / PRACTICAL APPROACH

How I would handle real community situations.

These sample scenarios show the care and judgment I would bring to common moderation tasks. They are examples of my approach, not claims of previous client projects.

SCENARIO 01

Responding to suspicious activity

If a suspicious link or possible impersonation appeared, I would follow the team's security procedures, report or restrict the content when authorized, and alert the relevant team member. I would prioritize member safety and avoid presenting unverified claims as facts.
